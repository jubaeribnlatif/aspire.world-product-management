01 / THE WORLD YOU'RE STEPPING INTO
Small things nobody puts in a brochure.
The practical details that tend to catch first-year students off guard, not a general portrait of the country.
"Is a French public university still almost free?"
Tap to reveal
Actually
Not for most new non-EU students. A decree of 19 May 2026 sets 2026–27 fees at public universities at €2,902 a year for a bachelor's and €3,950 for a master's, against €178 and €255 for others. Scholarship holders are exempt automatically, and institutions can exempt others on request up to a cap of 30% of non-EU students this year. Private schools set their own fees.
"Can I get by in English?"
Tap to reveal
Actually
For many master's programmes, yes: Campus France says there are over a thousand programmes taught fully or partly in English, and that a French level (at least DELF B2) is only needed if the programme is taught in French. Daily life still runs in French, so start learning before you land. The Campus France Bangladesh office sits inside the Alliance Française de Dhaka.
"How much can I work on a student visa?"
Tap to reveal
Actually
Up to 964 hours a year, which the guides describe as 60% of the legal annual working time, or about 20 hours a week if spread evenly. You need no separate work permit, but internships don't count toward the cap, and going over can cost you your permit renewal.
"Will the housing allowance (APL) help with rent?"
Tap to reveal
Actually
Probably not. A decree published on 28 June 2026 means that from 1 July non-EU students lose APL unless they hold, or qualify for, a social-criteria scholarship, or work alongside their studies in an apprenticeship or professional activity. Budget your rent as if you will receive nothing.
"Is my visa enough once I land?"
Tap to reveal
Actually
Not quite. A student VLS-TS doubles as your first-year residence permit, but you must validate it online within 3 months of arriving, and the validation tax is €150. Older guides say €75. Renewal later is online, between 4 and 2 months before expiry.
"Do I need health insurance from home?"
Tap to reveal
Actually
No separate policy is needed to start. Affiliation to student social security is mandatory and free: after you enrol, you register at etudiant-etranger.ameli.fr with your passport, visa and enrolment certificate. It doesn't reimburse everything, so universities recommend a paid supplementary plan (a "mutuelle"). Check what your visa file asks you to show.

06 / THE RULES OF THE ROUTE
The French student visa, in plain language.
Bangladeshi applicants go through Campus France Bangladesh first, then apply for a long-stay visa that doubles as a residence permit. These are the numbers and deadlines that decide whether you qualify, plus the 2026 rule changes to read first.
FUNDS YOU HAVE TO SHOW
€877.50 a month
AFTER A FRENCH MASTER'S OR EQUIVALENT: UP TO 12 MONTHS TO LOOK FOR WORK OR START A BUSINESS
Each square is one month of the job-search or business-creation permit. It is valid for one year and cannot be renewed. It needs a professional licence, a master's-level degree or an equivalent French diploma, and you can apply for it from outside France within 4 years of graduating. To work on it, the job must relate to your studies and pay more than €2,800.53 gross a month. Source: Service-Public, verified 1 June 2026.
Dates: the Études en France campaign opened on 1 October 2026 and Campus France Bangladesh gives 18 November 2026 as the file deadline, after which programme choices are locked. It names the campaign 2026–2027 without naming an intake; other Campus France offices describe the October 2026 opening as the 2027/28 admissions. Direct admission (applying to a university yourself) runs year-round, but you must register on the platform at least 60 days before the intake.
Steps: register on Études en France and get your profile validated (up to a week), then pay BDT 12,170 through Alliance Française de Dhaka (non-refundable), choose programmes, and attend a pre-consular interview at the Campus France Bangladesh office in Dhanmondi. The office works by appointment only, at bangladesh@campusfrance.org.
Visa: students don't book online. After Campus France, you complete the France-Visas form and submit in person at VFS Global in Gulshan 2, Dhaka, where biometrics are taken, and the Embassy of France in Dhaka decides. France-Visas also mentions an interview with the visa section. The France-Visas fee page would not load for us, so confirm the visa fee there.
Funds: €877.50 a month, which is €10,530 for 12 months (about ৳1,455,700). It was €615 for more than twenty years until 1 August 2026, so guides written before then are out of date. The figure is now indexed to the minimum wage.
Documents: Campus France Bangladesh points to the French Embassy's student-visa page for the list, which we could not open. A Sorbonne language school's 2026 guide lists the visa form, a passport valid beyond your stay, photos, your acceptance letter, the Campus France receipt, proof of funds and accommodation, and proof of health cover or your intent to join social security.
After you land: validate your VLS-TS online within 3 months of arriving (€150 tax) and register for student social security. The status lets you work up to 964 hours a year without a separate permit. Renew online between 4 and 2 months before it expires (€150, or €180 if late); the prefecture checks that your studies are real and serious.
Checked against Campus France Bangladesh, Service-Public, France-Visas and the Ministry of Higher Education, 18 Oct 2026
Rule changes · 2026
Tuition, proof of funds and housing aid all changed this year.
A decree of 19 May 2026 ended the near-free rate for most new non-EU students at public universities: 2026–27 fees are €2,902 for a bachelor's and €3,950 for a master's, and doctoral fees stay national. From 1 August 2026 the funds you must show rose from €615 to €877.50 a month. From 1 July 2026 non-EU students without a social-criteria scholarship lost the APL housing allowance. Many online guides, including some written this summer, still show the old figures. We have not seen the full decree texts, so confirm amounts with your university and the French Embassy in Dhaka.

Scholarships worth actually applying for.
France's tuition is no longer nearly free for most new non-EU students, so scholarships and fee exemptions matter more than they did a year ago. The Eiffel scholarship's university deadlines fall this month and next.
