What Erasmus Mundus actually is
A master's degree jointly run by at least three universities in three different countries — you genuinely study in more than one. It's EU-funded, open worldwide (including Bangladesh), and there's no single application: each programme runs its own admissions on its own site. This tool helps you navigate that, one programme at a time.
