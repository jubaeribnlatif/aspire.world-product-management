About the Chevening Scholarship.
The UK government's flagship international scholarship, running since 1983. It funds a fully-funded one-year master's degree for future leaders, chosen for potential and impact rather than academic record alone — which is exactly why the essays in your tracker carry more weight than the form itself.
Full tuition, any eligible course
Monthly living stipend
Return flights to the UK
Arrival & departure allowance
Thesis & travel grants
Lifetime alumni network
One requirement worth checking before you invest 8 hours
Chevening also asks for a minimum of two years' work experience by the time your course starts — internships and substantial part-time work can sometimes count. If you're a recent or soon-to-be graduate without this, it's worth confirming below before you go further. Commonwealth Scholarship, also in your strong matches, has no such requirement.
Genuinely competitive — tens of thousands apply worldwide each year for around 1,500 awards. Confirmed against Chevening's published guidance, 3 Aug 2026.

A Chevening-ready CV, UK style
Keep it to 1–2 pages — UK reviewers expect concise, not exhaustive.
No photograph. Unlike common practice in Bangladesh, UK-style CVs omit photos entirely.
Reverse-chronological: most recent education or role first.
Order: Education → Work experience → Leadership & extracurricular → Skills.
Quantify achievements — "led a team of 5" beats "worked in a team."
Drop "References available on request" — just omit references entirely.
Lead with leadership and community impact — it's what Chevening panelists weigh most.
Target submission: 11 Nov 2025 — 3 days before the actual deadline. Chevening's portal gets heavy traffic in the final 48 hours.
