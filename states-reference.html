Empty, error & loading states
Canonical patterns for every surface on aspire.world — reuse these rather than inventing new treatments per page. Each follows the same voice: calm, specific, never blaming the user.
Empty states
When there's genuinely nothing to show yet — never treat this as an error.

Specific about what went wrong and what to do next — never a bare "something went wrong."
