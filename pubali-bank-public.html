About
Pubali Bank PLC
Pubali Bank is one of Bangladesh's largest private commercial banks, with a nationwide branch network and a real-time centralized online banking system. As aspire.world's title financial partner, Pubali supports students through the Foreign Education Loan and Student File services described below.

Eligibility & your documents
The specific criteria Pubali checks — and which documents you already have in your aspire.world wallet.
WHO QUALIFIES
Applicant must be the parent or first relative bearing the student's educational expenses.
Applicant must be a Bangladeshi individual, supported by NID.
Minimum monthly net income should be at least 3× the monthly EMI.
For loans over ৳5,00,000, the applicant must be a registered taxpayer.
Applicant's age must not exceed 65 at the time the loan matures.
No hidden charges — full terms disclosed before you sign anything.
